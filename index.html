Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers Before You Book

How do you calculate the rental price?

Our rate is simple: flat ₹100 per square foot. For example, an 8×12 ft display (96 sq ft) costs ₹9,600 for the screen rental. Transport is free within 10 km, and calculated fairly according to distance and equipment size beyond 10 km.

What power supply is required?

Power depends on screen size: an 8×12 ft screen requires 3 kW to 5 kW, while an 8×24 ft screen requires 5 kW to 8 kW. Generator or stable main line power must be supplied at the venue.

How long does installation take?

Setup takes approximately 1.5 hours for an 8×12 ft screen and up to 3 hours for an 8×24 ft screen, depending on venue accessibility and stage design complexity.

Can you install the screen the day before the event?

Yes. If your Sunday event requires setup on Saturday, we can complete prior-day setup. The client covers extra vehicle holding, crew night stay, and food expenses.

What is your rain and bad weather policy?

For rain conditions, setups must be situated under a proper covered stage where direct rainwater does not enter. If minor splashes occur on cabinet panels, our team covers and protects them immediately.

What is your booking advance and cancellation policy?

We require a 30% advance payment to reserve your event date. Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior to the event receive a full refund or full credit transferred to your next scheduled event.

Do you provide live camera feeds or stage risers?

Yes. We offer live camera feeds using a high-end Sony PXW-Z90 4K camera at highly competitive add-on rates. We also supply pre-fabricated or custom-built stage risers for an additional fee.
