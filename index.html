Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers Before You Book

How do you calculate the rental price?

Our rate is flat ₹100 per square foot. For example, an 8×12 ft display (96 sq ft) costs ₹9,600 for screen rental. Transport is calculated based on distance from Dalongghat, Darrang.

Is Kankan LED Wall a registered entity?

Yes. Kankan LED Wall (Legal Name: Kankan Deka) is a GST registered business (GSTIN: 18DREPD3885A1ZY) located in Kawaimari, Dalongghat, Darrang, Assam.

What power supply is required?

An 8×12 ft screen requires 3 kW to 5 kW, while an 8×24 ft screen requires 5 kW to 8 kW. Generator or stable main line power must be supplied at the venue.

What is your booking advance and cancellation policy?

We require a 30% advance payment to reserve your event date. Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior receive a full refund or full credit transferred to your next scheduled event.
